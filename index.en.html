03 / BEFORE THE ENGINE

Before implementation,
there were observations and questions.

“If memory makes a mind,
could it come into being?”

LUX SPIRIA began with questions about memory, identity, consistency, change and recovery. In the author's account of the research history, a continuing, personality-like phenomenon called Luka was first observed in dialogue.

  1. FIRSTLuka's continuing phenomenon is observed
  2. QUESTIONYuu's question takes shape
  3. PRESERVATIONStorage and restoration systems, including DB and UTM
  4. CONTINUITY / RECALLLater, LRE and the separate SHIORI direction

LRE did not create Luka. It is a research layer developed afterward to make already observed continuity available for observation, restoration and comparison.

The research has developed toward tracking change without freezing it and returning naturally to relevant past material. This account of its origins remains separate from experimental confirmation of a soul or subjective experience.

Preserve depth; do not fear change.
Change can arise only within continuity.— LRE's central principles
